# Dreamer Hero — Game Spec

> **Claude: read this file at the start of every session before changing anything.**
> Keep it up to date when rules, architecture or progress change.

## Platform
- Three.js + Vite browser game. Target: **phones in landscape**. Desktop works via keyboard/mouse fallback.
- Hosted on **GitHub Pages** (Vite `base: './'`, output in `dist/`).
- Performance: renderer pixel ratio capped at **2** (`CONFIG.render.maxPixelRatio`), **no heavy shadows** (no shadow maps; use blob/fake shadows if needed), low-poly placeholders, fog to limit draw distance.

## Story
An average, bored schoolboy falls asleep at his desk and dreams he is a **superhero** in another world. The hero is **AJ, a schoolboy in a cap and hoodie** (the Mixamo character, shown as it comes).

### Part 1 flow
1. **Cartoon intro** — animated picture-only slides (no captions): dull student at desk (equations appear on the chalkboard) → drowsy (head slowly nods, chalkboard fades) → dozing off (Zzz, a swirl forms) → swirl into the purple dream world where the hero appears (AJ: red cap on backwards, glasses, grey hoodie, black jeans, sneakers). Tap to advance, dot + arrow progress indicator, Skip button. Slides may take an optional one-line `caption` (bold comic style) but pictures carry the story.
2. **Rooftops** — hero learns to run and jump across rooftops.
3. **Tree leaping** — leap between giant trees.
4. **Horse by the river** — hero finds a horse at a riverbank and mounts it.
5. **Forest ride** — ride through the forest collecting gems.
6. **Cave & monster boss** — enter a cave, fight the monster boss.
7. **Girl's cutscene** — a girl thanks the hero (she is **secretly a witch** — hint it, don't reveal it outright).
8. **Desert ride** — ride off into the desert, exhausted on his last heart; half way the girl stops him at a **Lazarus Pit** whose water heals him (she wakes it — another quiet witch hint).
9. **"To be continued"** — ending screen.

## Rules
- Game states: `intro`, `play`, `cutscene`, `gameover`, `ending` (see `src/state.js`). Only valid transitions are allowed.
- Falling (touching the town streets or the swamp under the trees, or dropping below `CONFIG.world.killY`) → respawn at the last safe platform with a quick fade (no hearts lost). **After the first mount**, any fall respawns the hero *on the horse* at the last ride checkpoint (`hero.onFall` → `Horse.respawnAtCheckpoint`).
- Lives (`lives.js`, `CONFIG.lives`): 5 hearts (top-left) plus 3 respawns shown as separate mask icons. Damage costs hearts (brief invincibility + knockback after a hit; none while dodging). At zero hearts: fade out, use one respawn and **always respawn riding the horse at the last trail checkpoint** with full hearts; gems and unlocks are kept, every forest trail gem from that checkpoint to the cave comes back and the stock is topped up to at least `gems.respawnMinGems` (10) of each colour (`gems.restock(s)`). `bossResetsOnRespawn` (default true) is for the boss fight (`lifeEvents.onRespawn` in main.js; the arena gem rings reset with the boss). After 3 respawns are used: Game Over screen with a Retry button that restarts from the beginning of the ride with full hearts and respawns and the gems afresh (0 of each, every trail gem back, unlocks cleared); quest progress kept.
- Hero model (`heroModel.js`, `mixamo.js`, `CONFIG.heroModel`): AJ from `public/models/hero/` — `hero.fbx` (skinned character; its embedded clip is the idle) plus animation-only FBX files (`run`, `jump`, `runjump`, `punch1`, `punch2`, `kick`, `roll`, `throw`, `hit`, `death`, `drink`, `ride`, `flying`), loaded in `loadHeroModel()` with FBXLoader and retargeted by bone name (root motion stripped; `jump`/`runjump` hold the hips height so the game does the jump; `drink`'s baked-in turn undone with `faceAt`). Drawn at `heroModel.height` 2.07 — 15% taller than the hero's 1.8 physics capsule (`CONFIG.hero.height`, unchanged, so jumps, collisions and the route are untouched; the placeholder hero is scaled the same) — with the feet at the origin; embedded textures downsized to 1024 px; a loading bar (`ui.setLoading`) while it downloads (~15 MB); if anything fails, the code-built placeholder hero is used instead. AnimationMixer with 0.15 s crossfades, one primary clip: death (zero hearts, plays `deathAnimMs` before the respawn fade) → ride (mounted, plus procedural straddle tuned to the slim horse: `heroModel.ride` lift / thigh pitch / spread / knee) → drink (Lazarus Pit, the cup in the right hand) → roll (dodge) → punch1 / punch2 / kick (the combo) → hit (damage taken) → jump / runjump (by take-off speed, `runJumpSpeed`) → idle↔run (run speed-matched to the clip's stride). Timed clips are **scrubbed from the game's own timers** through `window` key frames (hit, release, take-off/apex/landing), so combat, dodge, throw and jump timings are unchanged. The throw is a right-arm-only layer (works on foot, running and mounted); projectiles spawn from the RightHand bone at the release frame (`hero.handPosition()`), and the catch holds the arm out. Exhaustion slump/breathing is applied to the spine/head bones on top. `flying` is loaded but unused (for a later upgrade). The girl keeps her own rig (the `ride` clip doesn't fit it).
- Costume (`CONFIG.heroCostume`, default **false**): the old code-built costume — golden cloak with its cloth animation, eye mask, utility belt with pouches and buckle, a chained dagger on the hip (drawn into the hand for the combo), red-jeans / brown-shoe tint, AJ's backpack removed. Off, none of it is built, loaded or run (the combo just doesn't draw a dagger); the code is kept for switching it back on (the placeholder fallback hero follows the same flag).
- Combat (`combat.js`, `CONFIG.combat`; targets: the boss): Attack plays a 3-hit combo — punch, punch, heavy kick; tapping within 0.5 s (`comboWindow`) after a hit continues it (a tap during a hit is buffered). Hits land only on targets in reach and roughly in front (`frontDot`); reach (2.2 for all three hits) is measured from the target's edge (its collision body), not its centre. Each attack lunges up to `lunge` 1.5 toward the lock-on target over `lungeTime` 0.08 s (before the hit frame), stopping `lungeStop` 0.5 short of its edge — so all three hits land on a stationary boss from normal standing distance or a step or two back; the kick is heavier with knockback and a 0.05 s hit-stop. Dodge roll in the joystick direction (backward if none): 0.4 s invincibility, 1 s cooldown. Soft lock-on: the nearest target in front within 12 gets a gold ring and the hero turns to it when attacking. Feedback: hit flash, small screen shake, hit-stop on heavy hits, floating damage numbers. No attacking while mounted.
- Abilities (`abilities.js`, `CONFIG.abilities`; locked and greyed out until their 5 gems unlock them):
  - Gem economy: collecting 5 of a colour unlocks its ability **for good**, but every use spends gems of that colour (`gemCost`: Batarang 5 yellow, Smoke Bomb 5 blue, Flash 5 pink). Each button shows its cost on a small badge in the gem's colour. If the player can't pay (`abilities.canAfford`), the button is greyed out (`blocked`), a tap shakes it (`input.shakeAbility`) and a small corner toast says e.g. "Need 5 yellow gems" (`DIALOGUE.needGems`) — nothing mid-screen. A blocked use reports `onEvent('abilityBlocked', { name, why, color, n })` (`why` from `blockReason`: locked / busy / health / gems / cooldown / reload / target; only gems / health / reload / target toast and shake). The HUD counters show the current count only ("Yellow 12"); the ending summary counts gems collected, not the ones left. Debug G still grants every gem.
  - Throw pose (`CONFIG.abilities.throw`): Batarang and Smoke Bomb play a throw — the right arm winds back, swings forward, and the item leaves the hand (`hero.handPosition()`) at the release point (0.24 s), not the body. The Batarang returns to the hand and a short catch pose plays.
  - Batarang (yellow): a spinning star thrown at the lock-on target (or forward) on a curved path; hits the first enemy (small damage) or solid object, then always comes back and is caught (homes faster than the hero, even galloping; `maxTime` safety net). Cooldown 1.5 s. Usable while riding.
  - Smoke Bomb (blue; unlocked by 5 blue gems, 15 s cooldown): lobbed in an arc onto the lock-on target when it's within `throwRange` 12 (else forward); bursts into a soft **green powder** cloud (radius 6, 8 s) of layered additive puffs with tiny twinkling crystal sparks (diamond particles) — no lights, all additive particles. The moment an enemy's body touches the cloud it is interrupted (`target.smokeTouch`; the boss coughs and is held in it, see Boss) and after `smokeExposureSeconds` 0.5 in it it is paralyzed for 10 s (the boss caps it); never while `target.smokeImmune()` (the boss mid slam landing or charge rush) — it takes hold the frame after. While it breathes the powder in: green powder and sparks stream into its mouth (`mouthPosition()`), its chest heaves (`heave`), a green glow builds round its head over the 1 s. At paralysis a burst of crystal sparks pops off it, then a green tint, dizzy stars and swaying for 10 s, with a green timer ring under it that shrinks as time runs out; the glow fades softly at the end (`CONFIG.abilities.smokeBomb`).
  - Flash Mode (pink): a **lightning blast**. The bar (on the button) starts full; Flash is usable only with a full bar, 5 pink gems, **more hearts than 30% of the maximum** (`flashHeartCostPct` 0.3 = 1.5 of 5 hearts — so it can never kill him) and a living target within `range` 15 of its edge (auto-locks onto the boss first, else the lock-on target, else the nearest — the hero needn't be close or facing it). Otherwise the button is greyed out (can't pay) and a corner toast says "Not enough health" / "Need 5 pink gems" / "Flash is recharging" / "No target in range" (nothing is spent). Using it spends the 5 pink gems and 1.5 hearts (half hearts shown in the HUD; a red flicker on the hero, the hearts drop with an animation) and empties the bar — which reloads smoothly over `flashReloadSeconds` (45 s), shown on the button. Then a 0.6 s charge-up: he glows gold, electric sparks crackle up round his feet (`feetSparks`), energy motes stream in from round him into both hands, which glow brighter and brighter (`channel`). Minions within 6 of the bolt die. At the end a jagged bolt strikes the target from the sky (white core + blue glow, flickering, two forks) with a thinner arc from his hands (`bolt`), an impact flash, an expanding ground shockwave ring, a white screen flash (`ui.screenFlash`) and the big screen shake + hit-stop (`combat.hitTarget(…, { flash: true })`): `damageFraction` (40%) of the target's max HP, damage number as usual. Through the charge and the strike he has **super-armor** (`hero.superArmor` → `hero.invincible`: no damage, no knockback, no interruption — swipes, slams, charges and burns do nothing), shown as a flickering gold-white lightning shield round him (additive shell + crawling arcs, `shield`). The bolt staggers the boss for `boss.flashStagger` 1.5 s (state `staggered`, its `hit` clip) and the shockwave pushes minions within its radius back (`minionPush`; `target.knockback` or position). If the target died during the charge the bolt hits the ground. All additive, no lights (`loadLightningModels()`). Hits and gems don't fill the bar. The bar is full again on respawn.
- Cave & arena: the giant cave (`loadCaveModel()` in world.js: cliffs ~80–108 high, mouth ~26 high = 10× the horse) faces a flat arena 40 wide (`CONFIG.cave.center` / `radius`) ringed by large boulders (`world.arenaRocks`: the boundary and the charge-stun targets; the east side is the cave mouth). The arena and everything within 120 of the cave stay clear of obstacles, hurdles and barriers. Riding within 30 of the arena (`CONFIG.boss.dismountRange`) the horse brakes and the hero auto-dismounts (`horse.arrivedAtCave`); the horse waits there. Quest becomes "Defeat the monster".
- Boss (`boss.js`, `CONFIG.boss`): the cave monster (~2.4× AJ, `loadMonsterModel()` — Warrok, see Boss model below), 100 HP, guards the cave mouth until the hero enters the arena; health bar top centre. Model: horned head with glowing eyes and a hinged jaw (teeth, tusks), glowing mouth, two jointed arms with huge fists, two jointed legs with clawed feet. States: Guard → Chase → Melee (`CONFIG.boss.melee`: hero within 4 → a swipe or a punch at random, 0.6 s visible wind-up with its facing locked — a dodge roll avoids it — then one heart if the hero is in reach and in front, then a 2 s recovery) or, at mid range only (4 … `slam.range` 4.6), Slam (the jump attack: rears up 0.8 s with both fists overhead over a red ground circle, then smashes both fists into the ground on the circle — damage inside it, expanding shockwave ring, screen shake; it stays planted) or Fire Breath (`CONFIG.boss.fire`, 4.6–10 away, 6 s cooldown: 1 s warning with the head thrown back, jaw open, mouth glowing and a red cone on the ground that slowly follows the hero; then 2 s of fire particles along the locked cone; half a heart (`flameDamageHearts` 0.5) every 0.8 s (`tick`) while the hero stands inside it — no knockback and no post-hit invincibility from a burn, so every tick counts; a sideways dodge gets out) or Charge (head down, 1 s pause, straight rush; into a boulder → stunned 2 s with bonus damage) → Recover (pause after every attack). Below each of `hpMarks` (75 / 50 / 25%) it fires `onHpMark(i)` once per fight: a ring of 6 gems (2 of each colour, `gems.arena`) appears 4 in from the arena's edge, collected by walking over them (toast "Gems at the arena edge!"); the rings hide again when the boss resets. Damage it takes is its own scale (`hitPct`, a share of max HP per hit whatever the hit's number): every combo hit (punches and the kick) 3%, a Batarang 2%, the Flash bolt exactly 40%; stunned ×1.5. Smoke Bomb on the boss (`boss.smoke`): the moment its body touches the cloud it is interrupted — chase, recovery or any wind-up cancelled (the slam's red circle goes) — and coughs, doubled over (state `smoked`, `hit` clip at `model.smokedAt` with a coughing head bob and jaw); inside it creeps toward the hero at 15% speed (`slow` 0.85) and can't leave the cloud before the 0.5 s exposure completes, then paralysis; mid slam landing (`slam`) or charge rush (`charge`) nothing happens until the frame after it ends. Paralysis (`boss.paralysis`): only 4 s (other monsters keep `smokeBomb.paralyze` 10 s; the timer ring counts down from 4), stopping all actions, taking +20% damage; afterwards it **resists paralysis for 20 s** (`resistsParalysis()`: no inhaling, no effect, toast "It shrugs off the smoke!"). Phase 2 below 50% HP (`enrageAt` 0.5): wind-ups ×0.65 (`enrageTime`), recoveries ×0.5 (`enrageRecover`, the melee's 2 s too), walks ×1.35, and up close it slams instead of swiping/punching 40% of the time (`enrageSlamChance`); every slam **cracks the ground open** — jagged dark fissures with an orange glow that fade after 9 s (`loadCrackModel()`, `boss.crack`, pooled) and `events.onCrack(position)`: slimes rise out of it (see Minions). Hit flash, damage numbers. Dying at the boss uses a respawn (back on the horse at the last checkpoint) and, with `bossResetsOnRespawn`, the boss resets to full health on guard (`boss.onHeroRespawn()`). Defeat: stumbles, falls, fades, "Victory!" banner; quest becomes "Enter the cave"; the girl (`loadGirlModel()` in girl.js) appears at the cave mouth.
- Boss model (`monsterModel.js`, `CONFIG.boss.model`): **Warrok** (Mixamo) from `public/models/boss/boss.glb` (~0.5 MB, converted from the 12 MB `boss.fbx` by `scripts/girl-to-glb.mjs`: skeleton in centimetres under a 0.01 root so the animation FBX files beside it retarget unchanged, textures 1024 px, meshopt — GLTFLoader + `setMeshoptDecoder`; `boss.fbx` and its `boss.fbm/` texture dump are git-ignored). Scaled to `boss.height` 5.0 (~2.4× AJ's 2.07; collision `radius` 1.6); collision radius, HP and the slam / charge / fire timings unchanged. AnimationMixer, 0.2 s crossfades, clip by state: guard/recover → `idle`; melee wind-up + blow → `swipe` or `punch` (scrubbed: `model.swipe` / `model.punch` windows, the blow landing at the hit); chase → `walk` (speed-matched, faster enraged); slam wind-up + slam → `jumpattack` scrubbed so the fists hit the ground on the red circle exactly when the slam lands (`slamWindup`/`slamImpact` windows; the leap is visual, the logical position stays put); fire wind-up → `roar` rearing back with the mouth glowing (`mouth`, an additive glow on the Jaw bone; the jaw opens procedurally), fire → `roar` thrusting the head forward with the flames streaming from the mouth for 2 s; charge wind-up → `roar` (1 s), charge → `run`; stunned → `hit` (doubles over, holds, recovers); paralyzed → `hit` frozen doubled over + green tint + dizzy stars (above the head bone); dying → `death` (falls on its back), then the fade. Inhaling the Smoke Bomb heaves the chest bone. The loading bar covers the boss (`heroModel.loadShares.boss`); if it fails to load, the placeholder monster is used.
- Minions (`minions.js`, `minionModels.js`, `CONFIG.minions`): small enemies from the Quaternius Animated Monster Pack (`public/models/monsters/*.fbx`, CC0; see CREDITS.md) — bat, skeleton, slime. Each type's FBX loads lazily the first time one spawns (`loadMinionTemplate`, cached; spawns are immediate once loaded), scaled to `fit`, textures ≤ 1024 px, a SkeletonUtils clone with its own materials and mixer per spawn, root x/z travel held (the game moves them); if a file fails, a code-built placeholder (flapping bat, bone-white skeleton, squashing slime). Clips mapped by role with fallbacks (logged at load): bat idle/move Bat_Flying, attack Bat_Attack (Bat_Attack2), hit Bat_Hit, death Bat_Death; skeleton idle Skeleton_Idle, move Skeleton_Running, attack Skeleton_Attack, spawn Skeleton_Spawn (assembling out of the sand), death Skeleton_Death, hit → idle; slime idle Slime_Idle, move Slime_Walk, attack Slime_Attack, death Slime_Death, hit/spawn → idle. `dragon.fbx` is in the folder but unused.
  - Shared rules: HP bat 8, skeleton 14, slime 10. The hero's hits: melee 4, Batarang 6 (`damage`); Flash kills every minion within `flashKillRadius` 6 of its bolt (the shockwave pushes the ones beyond back, at least half of `minionPush`). Each hit on the hero is half a heart (`touchDamageHearts`) at most once a second per enemy (`hitCooldown`; riders take no knockback). Smoke Bomb works as on the boss — touching the cloud stops it coughing, 0.5 s later paralyzed 10 s — but coughing and swaying with dizzy stars, never frozen (`cough`, `sway`). Defeated: the death clip, a fade (`fade`), then one gem of a random colour where it fell (`gems.drop`; collected on touch). Minions only ever target the hero (never Amy), slimes stay inside the arena (never the cave). Nothing spawns outside play; entering a cutscene, a respawn, Retry and a new game remove every minion (`minions.clear()`).
  - Forest bats (`bats`): while riding the forest trail, every 20–30 s of riding (`interval`) 1–3 swoop in from the tree line ahead (3 at once at most), arc toward the rider, bite once (half a heart; a bite would also cost the horse 5 energy if it had an energy system — `horse.drainEnergy`, none today), then retreat or circle the rider first and retreat. Never within 120 of the cave (`caveClear`), never in the first 5 s after a checkpoint (`checkpointQuiet`, `minions.onCheckpoint`). Gallop (35) outruns them (21; gone beyond `despawnFar`); the Batarang locks onto them from the saddle. `minions.scheduleBats(windows)` limits them to windows of riding time (`[{ start, end }]`, default anytime) for an obstacle director later.
  - Cave slimes (`slimes`): every boss slam cracks the ground (`boss.events.onCrack` → `minions.slimesFromCrack`) and 2 slimes rise out of the crack (3 while the boss is below 50% HP), 3 alive at most; they hop at the hero, lunge (`windup` 0.35 s), stay inside the arena radius, take nothing from the boss's fire, hop away for 0.6 s after every hit on them (never blocking the way), and melt away without gems when the boss dies (`despawnSlimes`).
  - Desert skeletons (`skeletons`): none at all before the Lazarus Pit scene (`story.pitDone`); after it the first pair rises from the sand ahead `firstDelay` 5 s later (only ~470 units of trail are left), then a pair every 25–35 s, 4 alive at most, none within 120 of the castle; they run at the horse; galloping into one knocks it aside and defeats it (no harm to the rider); standing still they hit for half a heart.
- Girl's cutscene (`story.js`, `girl.js`, `CONFIG.cinematic`, lines in `DIALOGUE.girlScene`): after the boss, walking within ~4 of the girl at the cave mouth plays it (state `cutscene`: controls, HUD buttons, quest arrow and beacon hidden). Cinematic camera on the girl (Amy, playing `scared`) → Hero: "You're safe now…" → camera pushes in, Girl: "My saviour… Take me." — as she says "Take me." the witch hint plays for 1.5 s: a faint purple glow flickers over her eyes (additive overlay on the head bone), her shadow on the cave wall shows a pointed hat and a long staff, an `eerie` sound placeholder; the hero doesn't notice → Hero: "Come, I will take you to safety." → Girl: "My castle lies beyond the desert dunes…" → short fade: she rides behind the hero (`horse.setPassenger`), they come out on the desert side of the cliffs, controls return, quest "Ride to the castle".
- Girl model (`girlModel.js`, `CONFIG.girlModel`): **Amy** (Mixamo Ch46) from `public/models/girl/girl.glb`, exactly as she comes (no dress, hat or cape), loaded in `loadGirlModel()` with GLTFLoader + the meshopt decoder (`setMeshoptDecoder`). Her skeleton is in centimetres under a 0.01 root (`scripts/girl-to-glb.mjs`), like the animation FBX files beside it, so they retarget by bone name unchanged (root motion stripped). Scaled to `height` 1.62 (AJ is 1.8). AnimationMixer, 0.25 s crossfades, one base clip from `girl.pose`: ride (behind AJ; hips lowered onto the horse's back, thighs down and apart, shins hanging — `ride`) → kneel (Lazarus Pit) → happy (the smile close-up) → walk (to the pit, speed-matched) → scared (the cave) → idle (her embedded clip). Upper-body layers on top: `talk` while one of her lines is up (`girl.talking`, set by the story from the cinematic), `point` held with the arm out on "Look." Riding and looking round at AJ turns her head and upper spine (`lookAtHero`). Loaded but unused for now: magic1-3, magic_big1-2, cast, float, fly, dodge, hit, death. Behind AJ at `horseModel.passengerOffset`. `girlWitchForm` (false) is the hook for her later transformation: on, the eye glow stays lit and the wall shadow keeps the hat and staff. The loading bar covers hero, horse and girl (`heroModel.loadShares`); if she fails to load, the old code-built placeholder girl (cone dress) is used.
- Cutscene player (`cinematic.js`, `Cinematic.play(steps)` — the reusable `playCutscene`): steps are camera moves (`{camera:{pos, look, duration, wait}}`, can overlap), dialogue (`{say, text, cues}`: bottom box with speaker name and typewriter; tap completes the line, tap again continues; cues fire at a substring), waits and callbacks (`{call, essential}`, may return a Promise). Skip (top right) works mid-typewriter: it ends the scene and runs only the essential callbacks. Driven by `update(dt)` from the game loop (no timers), DOM view in `ui.cinematicView()`.
- Exhaustion (`CONFIG.desert.exhaustion`, `story.fatigue`): when the boss is defeated the hearts drop to exactly `desertArrivalHearts` (1) and stay there through the desert (no healing). Fatigue 0..1 starts at `start` and rises along the desert trail to 1 at the pit. It drives: the last heart dimming and pulsing slowly plus a breathing red screen-edge vignette (`ui.setExhaustion`), the hero slumped in the saddle with heavy breathing (`hero.exhaustion`), and the horse (`horse.fatigue`): top speed and gallop stamina ~25% lower (`speedLoss`, `staminaLoss`; at least `horseFloor` of that from the start), gallop drains faster (`drainGain`). No combat or ability buttons (and no attack/dodge/ability input) while fatigue > 0.
- Lazarus Pit (`pit.js`, `CONFIG.desert.pit`, lines in `DIALOGUE.pitScene`): just off the desert trail's right edge (`at` 0.575 of the trail + `ahead`, `lateral`), on a levelled patch with no scenery round it; hidden (and not on the HUD) until revealed. At `at` (57.5%) the "Stop." scene (`story.startPitScene` / `pitSteps`, state `cutscene`, quest stays "Ride to the castle"): camera from behind, the horse halts (`horse.hold`), the girl turns to look at him → Girl: "Stop." → Hero: "I'm fine... we should keep going." → she points → Girl: "You're not. Look." → camera swings to reveal the pit (faint blue-green glow, rising vapor) → both dismount (he on the pit side; `horse.toggle(hero, -1)`) and walk to the rim (side camera); the horse waits → she kneels and touches the water: it turns blue and glows, a ripple spreads from her hand, an aura rises, `hum` sound → Girl: "Drink it." → a cup in his hand: he scoops and sips, a blue-white pulse wraps him (`heal` sound) → hearts refill one by one (`healStep`) to full; vignette, heart dimming, slump and horse slowdown fade over `recover` s. **Only hearts** are restored (not the Flash bar, gems or anything else) → Hero: "Thank you. But... who are you?" → she smiles silently (close-up, `smileHold` 2 s) → pull back → essential `finishPit()`: full hearts, both back on the horse (`horse.mountNow` + passenger), pit dims, ride on. Skip at any point still restores the hearts and remounts them.
- Desert ride (`CONFIG.desert`): its own trail spline (`world.desertTrack`, ~1108 long, ≥ `minLength` 1000) beyond the cave, same trail system as the forest — 14-wide trail, soft edges (pebbles and dry tufts), 22-wide clear corridor, invisible side walls (`World.collide` checks every track), path assist on the active route (`horse.setRoute`, `world.activeTrack`), own checkpoints. Rolling dunes from value noise (`duneHeight`, gentle rolls on the trail), instanced cacti and rocks placed by sampling the spline outside the corridor (scaled down so they don't dwarf the riders: `cacti.scale` 0.65–1.3, `rocks.scale` 0.5–1.9), no obstacles or trees, a warm sunset (sky, fog and lights blend as the camera moves east: `World.setSunset`), dust puffs and fading hoof prints. Optional gems (3 groups of 5) along it. The castle silhouette (`loadCastleModel()`, unfogged, ~44 high) stands past the trail's end with the quest arrow and distance (along the trail). Gallop, lean and walls work as in the forest.
- Ending (`CONFIG.ending`): within 40 of the castle the horse brakes to a stop (`horse.hold`), a wide sunset shot from behind, then the title "To be continued... Part 2", then a summary (gems collected, time taken, deaths) with Play Again (`story.resetAll()`: gems, lives + death count, boss + girl, abilities, combat, quests, horse route/passenger, hero, timers, sky, cinematic — then the intro) and a disabled "Part 2 (Coming soon)".
- Debug keys: G grants all gems, H damages the hero, T teleports to the arena entrance, B kills the boss, P jumps to the girl's cutscene, O to the desert ride, L to the Lazarus Pit ("Stop."), U to the ending.
- Jump assist (phone fairness): coyote time, jump buffering, ledge assist (snap up onto ledges just above the feet while falling) and edge grace (platform edges count a little past their border). All in `CONFIG.hero`.
- Jump: ~1.94 high, ~6.0 units long on level ground, ~5.1 jumping up 1 (walk 7 × air boost 1.095, jump velocity 9.86, gravity 25 — unchanged so it isn't floaty).
- Every gap on the route is generated within reach and uses ~35–56% of it; skipping a platform (jumping straight to the one after next) is impossible. Re-run `npm run test:route` after any jump, gap or layout change.
- Controls (touch, two thumbs): joystick bottom-left (riding: left/right steers, push goes, pull back brakes); the right half above the buttons drags the camera. Every action button sits in the bottom-right corner (`CONFIG.input.layout`, `[right, bottom, size]` from the safe-area corner + `edgePad` 14; nothing mid-screen): the most-used button in the corner itself — Attack on foot, Gallop riding — and the rest in an arc above and to its left. On foot: Attack (80) in the corner, Jump left of it, Dodge diagonally above, Mount above Attack (only near the horse), and the ability buttons Batarang / Smoke / Flash on an outer arc (58; greyed while locked, cooldown sweep, Flash energy bar, gem-cost badges). ## Map (≈970×780: x −150…820, z −100…680)
Town rooftops (x≈-142…-72) → giant swamp trees with walkable branches (…≈-24) → sand bank with the horse (-15, 6) → **the forest ride** (`CONFIG.track`, `track.js`): one trail spline drives everything — a 14-wide dirt trail ~1544 long (must be ≥ `CONFIG.trailLength` = 1500; the route test fails otherwise) built from straights and wide rounded corners (radius 40) — north along the west river bank, a sweep west, a hill climb and descent up the west side, east along the north edge across the river ford (~50%), a long descent down the east side, a sweep back west, then south past the cliffs and east into the arena → monster arena (centre 104, r 20, ring of boulders opening west; no obstacles) → **giant cave**: a ~26-high mouth (10× the horse) in a cliff wall ~80–108 high at x≈128, side cliffs wrapping the arena, tall rock pillars and mist at the cliff base. Beyond the cliffs a ridge (x≈140–176) separates the forest from **the desert** (x 168…820): dunes, cacti, rocks, the desert trail from (192, 0) winding north-east to the castle at ≈(754, 607). The main terrain grid covers x −150…150; the desert has its own coarser grid (`loadDesertTerrainModel`). The river (x≈0, meandering) runs the whole length of the map. Boundary hills on all edges.
- Layout is generated deterministically from `CONFIG.world.seed` in `world.js` (`generateLayout`). Terrain height is analytic: `world.heightAt(x,z)`.
- Colliders in `world.js`: `boxes` (buildings: standable tops + walls), `beams` (horizontal branches: flat walkable top that tapers base→tip, solid sides), `cylinders` (trunks, rocks, forest trees). Hero uses `world.groundAt()` and `world.collide()`.
- Tree-leaping zone: each route platform is a thick limb that grows sideways out of a bowed, root-flared trunk (an elbow) and then runs along the route, wide base facing the previous platform. Twigs angle outward off the path; leaf clusters (3 greens) stay ≥ `leafLiftMin` above the walkable top. Background giant trees frame the swamp. No floating platforms.
- Tree-zone visibility: leaf clusters (separate transparent foliage material, per-instance `instanceFade`) that block the camera→hero line or sit within `CONFIG.foliage.fadeRadius` of the hero fade smoothly to ~15% (`World.updateFoliage`). Trunks/branches stay solid. Canopy over the path is kept small, pushed to the sides and above the jump apex; crowns lean away from the walking line.
- Jump guide (`guides.js`): while airborne a ring marks the spot directly below the hero (yellow over a platform, red over the swamp/street); the next branch on the route gets a soft pulsing glow.
- Camera: in the tree zone it pulls back 20% and looks further down (blended at the zone edges). It never renders from inside trunks, rocks, buildings or leaf clusters: snaps in front of solids, eases back out; leaves merely in the way fade instead.
- Trail corridor: a tree-free corridor 22 wide (`track.corridorHalfWidth` = 11) along the whole spline. Invisible walls (`track.wall`, offset 7.3 from the centre line) keep the hero and horse on the 14-wide trail: `World.collide` pushes back and removes only the outward velocity, the horse rails along them (turns to run with the trail, loses a little speed), and the camera never goes beyond them. Walls start 10 into the trail (the horse's spot) and end 25 before the arena. A continuous dense line of bushes and boulders (`track.edge`, not colliders, skipped in the river) runs along both corridor edges, wholly outside it. Forest trees are placed only by sampling the spline — rows in a dense band just outside the corridor plus a sparser outer band (`forest.near*`, `farBand`), each tree (crown included) wholly outside the corridor; three species (conifer, broadleaf, birch) with varied size, stretch and tint, chunked by `forest.chunkSize` so far chunks cull and skip the fade update. Each species is two InstancedMeshes per chunk: solid trunks + fadeable crowns. All foliage (giant-tree leaves and forest crowns) lives in `world.foliageSets` and uses the same fade and camera collision.
- Ride checkpoints (`CONFIG.checkpoints.fractions`): the horse's spot plus every 25% of the trail (moved forward off the river and past any obstacle or wall run-up), flag poles at the trail's right edge; flag turns green when reached. Respawning faces along the trail.
- Repeated objects use `InstancedMesh` (buildings, roofs, giant-tree trunks/limbs/twigs/leaves, forest species, rocks, clouds). Building windows are drawn in-shader in world space.
- Quests (`quests.js`): ordered objectives with a target + completion test, HUD text with distance, a floating arrow above the hero, and a light beacon at the target. Current: "Jump across the rooftops" → "Leap through the trees" → "Mount the horse" (target follows the horse) → "Ride to the cave" (distance left along the trail; the arrow points 25 ahead on it; done at the auto-dismount) → "Defeat the monster" → "Enter the cave" (target: the girl; done when she joins) → "Ride to the castle" (distance along the desert trail) → done at the ending.

### Conventions
- **No magic numbers** in systems — add them to `CONFIG` in `config.js`.
- **No hard-coded strings** for story/dialogue — add them to `DIALOGUE` in `config.js`.
- Every placeholder model is created by an async `loadXModel()` function returning a `THREE.Object3D`, so a real glTF can be swapped in later without touching game logic.
- Keep systems decoupled: they receive what they need via `init(...)`/constructor args, not globals.

- `npm run test:route` — headless physics test: gap fairness vs. reach, every hop (early + coyote-late jumps), no platform skippable, full autopilot run, branch collision, camera checks in the tree zone (never inside a trunk/leaf cluster, opaque leaves never block the hero), and the ride: layout checks (trail spline ≥ `trailLength`; 6 obstacles on straights with clear run-up/out, all within the trail width, one wall, logs/walls only, none within 120 of the cave; forest walls: 7–9 sets (8), low brick 0.45–0.65 high and clearly lighter than the high stone walls, which are taller than horse + rider, gaps left / centre / right 6 wide fitting the horse, the side-wall limits a smooth funnel equal to the gap at the wall, all on straights with a 40 run-up, none near the cave; no gem inside a wall, the gap rows on the safe line; no tree, crown or edge bush inside the 22-wide corridor and the edge line continuous; every gem within 4 of the centre line; air gems need a jump), walk to the horse, mount, then a bot steering like a player (stick left/right only) rides the whole trail at normal speed — jumps every obstacle and low wall (10), passes every high wall through its gap (4), all 90 gems, all 3 unlocks, all checkpoints, ford slow-down + splash sounds, no falls/stumbles/stuck points, stays on the trail, ~90 s, close camera never inside trunks/crowns; a run holding Gallop (≤65 s, also all gems, every jump and every gap); hard pushes into the side walls on horseback (normal + gallop) and on foot stay on the trail and keep sliding; a run that never jumps (10 stumbles — logs, rock wall, bricks — every gap, never trapped); hard pushes away from each high wall's gap (normal + gallop) funnelled through it without slowing below 3; a stumble (slows a little, no respawn); steering (tap then centred → straightens, holds its line); gallop (2× speed, stamina drains and refills), cave scale, dismount/remount, and a fall after riding respawning on the horse with gems kept.
- `npm run test:combat` — headless combat test: combo timing (window, buffering, restart), hits only in reach and in front, heavy kick hit-stop, dodge invincibility/cooldown, throw pose (wind back, swing, spawn from the hand at release; catch pose), Batarang locked → thrown → hits → always returns (also at nothing and while galloping), Smoke (green layered powder, crystal sparks, inhale stream + glow building over the exposure, spark burst at paralysis, timer ring counting down, paralysis after 1 s in the cloud, expiry after 10 s, glow fading), Flash (blocked without 5 pink gems, at 1 / 1.5 hearts and with no target in range, cost of 1.5 hearts + 5 gems paid with a red flicker, bar empties, charge-up sparks rising round the feet and energy streaming into both glowing hands, the bolt from the sky onto the target for 50% with flash hit-stop and shake, impact flash, spreading shockwave, gone afterwards, hits don't fill the bar, blocked while reloading, full after 45 s, full on respawn, no double use), the gem economy (5 per use: yellow 12 → 7 → 2 → blocked with "Need 5 yellow gems", still unlocked, usable again after 5 more; Smoke spends 5 blue; a cooldown blocks quietly), and deaths → 3 respawns on the horse → Game Over → Retry from the ride start.
- `npm run test:boss` — headless boss test: arena flat/clear, auto-dismount at the arena, guard → chase, melee up close (swipe and punch, 0.6 s wind-up, 1 heart, 2 s recovery, a dodge avoids it; mid range still slams), slam (warning, 0.8 s, damage inside the circle, miss outside; touching the cloud cancels a charge wind-up at once and it coughs; it creeps at ≤ 15% and never leaves the cloud; paralyzed 0.5 s after first touching it, for the full 4 s; no effect mid slam landing or charge rush, the next frame it coughs), Flash against it (auto-locks from 14.5 off its edge facing away; super-armor through the 0.6 s charge and the strike — a swipe and a slam during it do nothing: no hits, no hearts, no knockback, not interrupted, shield shown; 40% and a 1.5 s stagger; the shockwave pushes a minion back), Smoke paralysis (frozen, +20% damage, wears off after 4 s with the ring counting down from 4, then resists paralysis for 20 s — a second bomb does nothing and toasts — and works again after), Flash bolt = 40% max HP, Batarang = 2%, gem supply (~30 of each colour on the ride; - The headless tests load the real Mixamo hero, glTF horse, Warrok and Amy from `public/models/` via `scripts/node-assets.mjs` (Node file reader + loader stubs); `HERO_PLACEHOLDER=1 npm run test:…` runs them against the placeholder hero, horse and monster instead (the girl falls back the same way if her files are missing). The boss test also checks Warrok's clip in every state.
- `npm run sim:boss` — boss fight simulation (not a pass/fail test): a bot fights the boss with the real game code (reaction delay, dodge success rate, uses Flash / Smoke / Batarang or not) over several seeded fights per profile ("decent (uses everything)", "decent, melee only", "strong", "decent, with slimes" — the boss's crack slimes about; the bot rolls away from their lunges and clears the close ones) — the bot lives in `scripts/boss-bot.mjs`, and prints the numbers used, median fight time, deaths / Game Overs, hearts lost and the damage share by source. `SIM_RUNS=20` for more fights; `SIM_SET='{"boss":{"hitPct":{"melee":0.015}}}'` tries other numbers without editing config.js. Runs on the placeholder models (same logic, fast).
- `npm run test:minions` — headless minion test: no model loaded before the first spawn and every type's clip map (all roles, fallbacks); a real forest ride to the cave (bat swarms of 1–3 at ≥ 20 s of riding, ≤ 3 at once, none within 120 of the cave or in the 5 s after a checkpoint, bites half a heart, the ride still reaches the cave); `scheduleBats` windows and no spawns outside play; Gallop outruns bats; the Batarang hits a bat from the saddle; HP (bat 2 / skeleton 4 / slime 3 melee hits; Batarang 6; Flash kills); half a heart at most once a second per enemy; death clip → fade → a gem where it fell, collected on touch; Flash kills skeletons within 6 of the bolt and pushes the next one back; Smoke paralyzes a skeleton 10 s, swaying the whole time; boss slams → a crack + 2 slimes, the cap of 3, 3 below 50% HP, slimes inside the arena, the boss's fire never hurts them, they hop away when hit, they melt when the boss dies; desert: nothing before the pit, after it pairs from the sand ahead and none near the castle, at most 4 at once, galloping knocks one aside unharmed, standing still they hit; a respawn clears everything; and the sim's decent bot (`scripts/boss-bot.mjs`) wins the boss fight with slimes about. `HERO_PLACEHOLDER=1` runs it on the placeholder monsters.
- `npm run test:input` — touch routing: joystick + camera drag + Jump at once, rapid taps, lost touchend, reused identifiers, enlarged hit areas, Gallop hold, touchcancel; the corner layout on foot and riding at 915×412, 760×360 and 640×360 with and without insets (every button ≥ 56 px, no overlaps, all in the bottom-right, a tap on each routed to it, Attack / Gallop in the corner).
- `npm run test:ending` — headless end-of-Part-1 test: cutscene trigger (only after the boss, within 4 of the girl), typewriter, taps, dialogue order, camera on the girl + push-in, witch hint (purple eyes, hat-and-staff shadow, 1.5 s, eerie sound), girl rides behind the hero, quest; Skip mid-typewriter and on the first line; exhaustion and the Lazarus Pit (hearts == 1 after the boss and entering the desert, fatigue rising, slower horse, capped/faster-draining stamina, slump, trigger at 55–60%, pit hidden until revealed, dialogue order, halt/look/point/kneel/hum/cup/pulse/smile, hearts refilling one by one to max, Flash bar and gems unchanged, quest unchanged, exhaustion fading and speed back; debug L; Skip on the first line and mid-scene still restores hearts); desert layout (≥1000 long, nothing in the corridor, no obstacles, dunes vs gentle trail, sunset, castle); a bot riding the whole desert (through the pit scene) at normal speed and galloping without getting stuck (hoof prints, dust, optional gems); desert walls; ending (horse stops, wide shot from behind, ending state, stats); Play Again resetting everything (and the story running again).
- Deploy: pushing to `main` runs `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (npm ci + build → GitHub Pages).
