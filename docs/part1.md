# Part 1 rules and systems (reference)
> Read the section you need; do not load the whole file.

### Part 1 flow
1. **Cartoon intro** — animated picture-only slides (no captions): dull student at desk (equations appear on the chalkboard) → drowsy (head slowly nods, chalkboard fades) → dozing off (Zzz, a swirl forms) → swirl into the purple dream world where the hero appears (AJ: red cap on backwards, glasses, grey hoodie, black jeans, sneakers). Tap to advance, dot + arrow progress indicator, Skip button. Slides may take an optional one-line `caption` (bold comic style) but pictures carry the story.
2. **Rooftops** — hero learns to run and jump across rooftops.
3. **Tree leaping** — leap between giant trees.
4. **Horse by the river** — hero finds a horse at a riverbank and mounts it.
5. **Forest ride** — ride through the forest collecting gems.
6. **Cave & monster boss** — enter a cave, fight the monster boss in a **sealed arena** (a rock barrier closes the entrance behind AJ until the boss is dead; only AJ fights, Amy watches from her corner, the horse waits inside).
7. **Girl's cutscene** — a girl thanks the hero (she is **secretly a witch** — hint it, don't reveal it outright).
8. **Desert ride** — ride off into the desert, exhausted on his last heart; half way the girl stops him at a **Lazarus Pit** whose water heals him (she wakes it — another quiet witch hint).
9. **Sandstorm chase** — from the pit on, a sandstorm rises and skeletons climb out of the sand and chase the horse to the castle.
10. **Castle gate** — a cinematic (not a fight): the storm clears, the horse gallops through a line of skeletons before the castle gates, and the gates slam shut behind them.
11. **The portal** — just inside the castle gate (the inner gates shut behind them, torches either side, sand underfoot) Amy gets down and turns to AJ; in a close-up (she looks normal — no glow, nothing revealed yet): "Thank you for bringing me home." — wide again, AJ: "Whoa... what's this place?" — mid-line a portal tears open beside him and at once pulls him and the horse in (AJ: "What's happening?!"); Amy reaches out to him, frozen, hand held out: "No......" as they vanish into the light; white → **Part 2**.


## Rules
- Game states: `intro`, `play`, `cutscene`, `gameover`, `ending` (see `src/state.js`). Only valid transitions are allowed.
- Falling (touching the town streets or the swamp under the trees, or dropping below `CONFIG.world.killY`) → respawn at the last safe platform with a quick fade (no hearts lost). **After the first mount**, any fall respawns the hero *on the horse* at the last ride checkpoint (`hero.onFall` → `Horse.respawnAtCheckpoint`).
- Lives (`lives.js`, `CONFIG.lives`): 5 hearts (top-left) plus 3 respawns shown as separate mask icons. Damage costs hearts (brief invincibility + knockback after a hit; none while dodging). Mounted, a hit (bat bite, skeleton, anything) never plays the `hit` clip, knocks back or moves him off the saddle: only a short seated jolt (`combat.riderFlinch`), a red flash that fades (`hurtRed`) and the 1 s invincibility shown as a soft opacity blink (`hurtBlink`) that always ends fully visible. These status timers tick in the hero's per-frame pose (`#animate`), so they run while riding too (main skips `hero.update` when mounted — the old blink froze half way and left AJ hidden); `hero.resetStatus()` clears invincibility, blink, flash and flinch on every respawn (also on the horse) and `die()` stops the blink. At zero hearts: fade out, use one respawn and **respawn riding the horse at the last trail checkpoint** with full hearts — except during the sealed boss fight, where he revives **inside the arena** on foot (`arena.reviveHero()`: full hearts, abilities refilled, a few seconds' protection — see Sealed arena; a fall there only puts him back inside, `arena.respawnHero()`); gems and unlocks are kept, every forest trail gem from that checkpoint to the cave comes back and the stock is topped up to at least `gems.respawnMinGems` (10) of each colour (`gems.restock(s)`). `bossResetsOnRespawn` (default true) applies to a respawn at the boss outside the sealed fight (`lifeEvents.onRespawn` in main.js; the arena gem rings reset with the boss); in the sealed fight the boss is not reset (see Sealed arena). After 3 respawns are used: Game Over screen with a Retry button that restarts from the beginning of the ride with full hearts and respawns and the gems afresh (0 of each, every trail gem back, unlocks cleared); quest progress kept.
- Hero model (`heroModel.js`, `mixamo.js`, `CONFIG.heroModel`): AJ from `public/models/hero/` — `hero.fbx` (skinned character; its embedded clip is the idle) plus animation-only FBX files (`run`, `jump`, `runjump`, `punch1`, `punch2`, `kick`, `roll`, `throw`, `hit`, `death`, `drink`, `ride`, `flying`), loaded in `loadHeroModel()` with FBXLoader and retargeted by bone name (root motion stripped; `jump`/`runjump` hold the hips height so the game does the jump; `drink`'s baked-in turn undone with `faceAt`). Drawn at `heroModel.height` 2.07 — 15% taller than the hero's 1.8 physics capsule (`CONFIG.hero.height`, unchanged, so jumps, collisions and the route are untouched; the placeholder hero is scaled the same) — with the feet at the origin; embedded textures downsized to 1024 px; a loading bar (`ui.setLoading`) while it downloads (~15 MB); if anything fails, the code-built placeholder hero is used instead. AnimationMixer with 0.15 s crossfades, one primary clip: death (zero hearts, plays `deathAnimMs` before the respawn fade) → ride (mounted, plus procedural straddle tuned to the slim horse: `heroModel.ride` lift / thigh pitch / spread / knee) → drink (Lazarus Pit, the cup in the right hand) → roll (dodge) → punch1 / punch2 / kick (the combo) → hit (damage taken) → jump / runjump (by take-off speed, `runJumpSpeed`) → idle↔run (run speed-matched to the clip's stride). Timed clips are **scrubbed from the game's own timers** through `window` key frames (hit, release, take-off/apex/landing), so combat, dodge, throw and jump timings are unchanged. The throw is a right-arm-only layer (works on foot, running and mounted); projectiles spawn from the RightHand bone at the release frame (`hero.handPosition()`), and the catch holds the arm out. Exhaustion slump/breathing is applied to the spine/head bones on top. `flying` is loaded but unused (for a later upgrade). The girl keeps her own rig (the `ride` clip doesn't fit it).
- Costume (`CONFIG.heroCostume`, default **false**): the old code-built costume — golden cloak with its cloth animation, eye mask, utility belt with pouches and buckle, a chained dagger on the hip (drawn into the hand for the combo), red-jeans / brown-shoe tint, AJ's backpack removed. Off, none of it is built, loaded or run (the combo just doesn't draw a dagger); the code is kept for switching it back on (the placeholder fallback hero follows the same flag).
- Combat (`combat.js`, `CONFIG.combat`; targets: the boss): Attack plays a 3-hit combo — punch, punch, heavy kick; tapping within 0.5 s (`comboWindow`) after a hit continues it (a tap during a hit is buffered). Hits land only on targets in reach and roughly in front (`frontDot`); reach (2.2 for all three hits) is measured from the target's edge (its collision body), not its centre. Each attack lunges up to `lunge` 1.5 toward the lock-on target over `lungeTime` 0.08 s (before the hit frame), stopping `lungeStop` 0.5 short of its edge — so all three hits land on a stationary boss from normal standing distance or a step or two back; the kick is heavier with knockback and a 0.05 s hit-stop. Dodge roll in the joystick direction (backward if none): 0.4 s invincibility, 1 s cooldown; it passes through slimes and through the boss's body, and against a rock or wall (`world.solidsNear`, `corner.dodge.wallReach`) it never rolls into it — the part of the direction into it is dropped, it bends away (`wallPush`) and gets a short push (`pushSpeed` for `pushTime`). Soft lock-on: the nearest target in front within 12 gets a gold ring and the hero turns to it when attacking. Feedback: hit flash, small screen shake, hit-stop on heavy hits, floating damage numbers. No attacking while mounted.
- Abilities (`abilities.js`, `CONFIG.abilities`; locked and greyed out until their 5 gems unlock them):
  - Gem economy: collecting 5 of a colour unlocks its ability **for good**, but every use spends gems of that colour: Batarang **1 yellow** per throw (`abilities.batarangGemCost`), Smoke Bomb 5 blue, Flash 5 pink (their `gemCost`); `abilityGemCost(name)` in config.js is the one place the code (abilities, tests) gets it from — the costs are **shown nowhere** in the interface. Each ability button shows a small badge in the gem's colour with the **uses left**: `abilities.usesLeft(name)` = gems of that colour ÷ its cost, rounded down (Batarang: yellow ÷ 1; Smoke Bomb: blue ÷ 5; Flash: pink ÷ 5), updated live after every use and pickup and capped at `input.usesBadgeMax` 99 on screen. At 0 uses (or while locked) there is no badge and the button is greyed out (`blocked`); a tap then only shakes it (`input.shakeAbility`) — no toast during the action, nothing mid-screen. The Flash button is also greyed while its bar reloads or the hero hasn't enough hearts. A blocked use reports `onEvent('abilityBlocked', { name, why })` (`why` from `blockReason`: locked / busy / health / gems / cooldown / reload / target; only gems / health / reload / target shake the button). The HUD counters show the current count only ("Yellow 12"); the ending summary counts gems collected, not the ones left. Debug G still grants every gem.
  - Throw pose (`CONFIG.abilities.throw`): Batarang and Smoke Bomb play a throw — the right arm winds back, swings forward, and the item leaves the hand (`hero.handPosition()`) at the release point (0.24 s), not the body. The Batarang returns to the hand and a short catch pose plays.
  - Batarang (yellow): a spinning star thrown at the lock-on target (or forward) on a curved path; hits the first enemy (small damage) or solid object, then always comes back and is caught (homes faster than the hero, even galloping; `maxTime` safety net). Cooldown 1.5 s. Usable while riding.
  - From the saddle all three abilities work (an arc of Batarang / Smoke / Flash above Gallop and Jump; only Attack and Dodge are hidden): the Batarang auto-aims (below), the Smoke Bomb is lobbed from the saddle onto the same auto-aim target (`mountedTarget()`: the nearest enemy of any type in any direction within `autoAimRange`, preferring ones in front), and Flash auto-locks onto the boss, else the nearest enemy (no lock-on ring while riding).
  - Batarang from the saddle (`batarang.mounted`): a tap auto-aims at the nearest living enemy of any kind (bats, skeletons, slimes, anything added later) within `autoAimRange` 25 in any direction — front, sides and behind — preferring ones in front (one right behind counts as `preferFront` 1.6× as far; within `overhead` 4 counts as in front), aiming at the middle of its hit body but never below `aimLift` above the ground (a skeleton still rising from the sand) — a small gold bracket (`bracket`, billboarded, `abilities.aimTarget`) marks it whenever the Batarang is ready; the target is kept from the tap to the release. It homes in at `homingSpeed` 30 on top of the rider's speed (measured from the hand) turning up to `turnRate` 540°/s, leaving a fading gold trail (`trail`, additive); no target → straight ahead along the heading (`forwardRange` 14). It turns back on a hit, when its target dies, or beyond `maxRange` 30, and returns on a tight turn-limited curve, caught however far the horse has gone; inside its own turning circle (`orbitGuard`) or off screen (`screenMargin`) it flies straight at the bat / the hand, so it never orbits or stays out of view. Against a bat it does 8 (`minions.types.bat.damageTaken`) = a kill. Hits use each enemy's hit body (`minions.types.*.hit`, a vertical capsule from its feet: a skeleton's is 1.2 wide × 2.1 tall, on the model, and it counts while it rises from the sand) with `hitPad` slack, swept along the flight so it never tunnels; on the way back it still strikes any enemy in its path (each at most once on the way back, `returnReach` more reach above a body since it flies back at hand height).
  - Smoke Bomb (blue; unlocked by 5 blue gems, 15 s cooldown): lobbed in an arc onto the lock-on target when it's within `throwRange` 12 (else forward); bursts into a soft **green powder** cloud (radius 6, 8 s) of layered additive puffs with tiny twinkling crystal sparks (diamond particles) — no lights, all additive particles. The moment an enemy's body touches the cloud it is interrupted (`target.smokeTouch`; the boss coughs and is held in it, see Boss) and after `smokeExposureSeconds` 0.5 in it it is paralyzed for 10 s (the boss caps it); never while `target.smokeImmune()` (the boss mid slam landing or charge rush) — it takes hold the frame after. While it breathes the powder in: green powder and sparks stream into its mouth (`mouthPosition()`), its chest heaves (`heave`), a green glow builds round its head over the 1 s. At paralysis a burst of crystal sparks pops off it, then a green tint, dizzy stars and swaying for 10 s, with a green timer ring under it that shrinks as time runs out; the glow fades softly at the end (`CONFIG.abilities.smokeBomb`).
  - Flash Mode (pink): a **lightning blast**. The bar (on the button) starts full; Flash is usable only with a full bar, 5 pink gems, **more hearts than 30% of the maximum** (`flashHeartCostPct` 0.3 = 1.5 of 5 hearts — so it can never kill him; **not in the sealed boss arena**, see below) and a living target within `range` 15 of its edge (auto-locks onto the boss first, else the lock-on target, else the nearest — the hero needn't be close or facing it). Otherwise the button is greyed out (no uses left, not enough hearts, or reloading) and a tap only shakes it — no toast (with no target in range it isn't greyed, but a tap likewise just shakes it); nothing is spent. Using it spends the 5 pink gems and 1.5 hearts (half hearts shown in the HUD; a red flicker on the hero, the hearts drop with an animation) and empties the bar — which reloads smoothly over `flashReloadSeconds` (45 s), shown on the button. Then a 0.6 s charge-up: he glows gold, electric sparks crackle up round his feet (`feetSparks`), energy motes stream in from round him into both hands, which glow brighter and brighter (`channel`). Minions within 6 of the bolt die. At the end a jagged bolt strikes the target from the sky (white core + blue glow, flickering, two forks) with a thinner arc from his hands (`bolt`), an impact flash, an expanding ground shockwave ring, a white screen flash (`ui.screenFlash`) and the big screen shake + hit-stop (`combat.hitTarget(…, { flash: true })`): `damageFraction` (40%) of the target's max HP, damage number as usual. Through the charge and the strike he has **super-armor** (`hero.superArmor` → `hero.invincible`: no damage, no knockback, no interruption — swipes, slams, charges and burns do nothing), shown as a flickering gold-white lightning shield round him (additive shell + crawling arcs, `shield`). The bolt staggers the boss for `boss.flashStagger` 1.5 s (state `staggered`, its `hit` clip) and the shockwave pushes minions within its radius back (`minionPush`; `target.knockback` or position). If the target died during the charge the bolt hits the ground. All additive, no lights (`loadLightningModels()`). Hits and gems don't fill the bar. The bar is full again on respawn. **In the sealed boss arena** (`abilities.inSealedArena`, set every frame from `arena.sealed`) Flash costs `flashHeartCostInArena` hearts (default 0: no spend, no red flicker) and needs at least `flashMinHeartsInArena` (default 0: any heart level — his last heart or half a heart — never greyed or blocked for health); only the pink gems (uses left) and the reload bar limit it there, and everything else (charge-up, bolt, 40% damage, stagger) is unchanged. Outside the arena (forest, desert, skeletons) it works exactly as above.
- Cave & arena: the giant cave (`loadCaveModel()` in world.js: cliffs ~80–108 high, mouth ~26 high = 10× the horse) faces a flat arena 40 wide (`CONFIG.cave.center` / `radius`) ringed by large boulders (`world.arenaRocks`: the boundary and the charge-stun targets; the east side is the cave mouth). The arena and everything within 120 of the cave stay clear of obstacles, hurdles and barriers. Riding within 30 of the arena (`CONFIG.boss.dismountRange`) the horse brakes and the hero auto-dismounts (`horse.arrivedAtCave`); the horse waits there. Quest becomes "Defeat the monster".
- Sealed arena (`arena.js` `SealedArena`, `CONFIG.arena`, toasts in `DIALOGUE.arena`): when the boss notices AJ (`boss.events.onNotice`: guard → chase as he steps in, or hit while on guard) the arena is armed; `barrierCloseDelay` 2 s later (once he is inside) a rock barrier (`loadBarrierModel()`: 5 rocks across the west opening between the entrance pillars) rises over `closeTime` with a rumble (`barrierRumble` sound placeholder), a camera shake and dust; its colliders go into `world.cylinders` (hero, camera, dodge all respect it; anything in the gateway is moved `clearIn` inside). It stays shut until the boss is dead, then sinks over `openTime` with another rumble and the colliders go. Only AJ fights. Amy (`boss.girlWatching`) is shown at her corner by the cave mouth (`boss.girl.position`, 20.7 from the centre, beyond the boss's leash + body) the whole fight, scared, only watching — no enemy ever targets her and nothing damages her; at the defeat she is already there (no appearing). The horse walks in when the boss notices AJ (`horseWalk`, a scripted walk via the gateway) to `horseSpot` inside by the entrance and grazes (the graze clip; the placeholder's head-down bob). The boss's fire hurts it: `horseFireDamage` 2 every fire `tick` while it stands in the cone, out of `horseMaxHealth` 10, with a small billboard health bar over it shown only once it is hurt (`healthBar`); at 0 it bolts round the arena's edge and fades out (`flee`, `horse.fled`: hidden, can't be mounted, `horseFlee` sound) and AJ fights on foot. When the barrier has opened after the fight the horse comes back to AJ by itself (back in through the opening if it fled, stopping `comeBack.stop` from him, health full). A death during the sealed fight (`arena.sealed`) still uses one of the 3 respawns (Game Over after them, unchanged) and revives AJ inside at `respawn` (`arena.reviveHero()`, main's death fade; `lifeEvents.onRespawn({ inArena: true })`), the horse back at its spot whole, the barrier still shut: **full hearts**; every ability refilled to at least `reviveUses` 5 uses (yellow 5, blue 25, pink 25 — more on hand is kept; unlocked) and the Flash bar full (cooldowns cleared); protected for `arenaReviveProtection` 3 s (`hero.protectFor` → `hero.reviveShield`: `hero.invincible`, a soft golden pulsing glow `reviveGlow` fading out at the end); the boss is **not reset** — `boss.reviveRecover(bossReviveRecoverFraction 0.5, …)` gives it back only half of the damage dealt so far (HP − its lost HP × 0.5 … e.g. 40 → 70 of 100), ends whatever it was doing and backs away (`boss.retreat`, its walk clip, `boss.retreat.speed`) to the far side of the arena opposite him (`farSide` 0.85 of its leash) and waits there facing him for the protection time, then fights on; the gem rings already shown stay. A faint golden glow (`loadAmyGlowModel()`, `amyGlow`: additive motes with a short trail) drifts in an arc from Amy's corner to him over ~1 s as he revives — she doesn't move or attack and stays safe. A fall in the sealed fight only puts him back inside (`respawnHero`, no hearts lost, nothing else changes). Boss regen (only in the sealed fight: main sets `boss.regenEnabled = arena.sealed` in play, false in a cutscene; never the minions, bats or skeletons): once nothing has hurt it for `bossRegenDelay` 4 s (`boss.sinceHit`, reset by every `takeHit` — combo, Batarang, Flash, anything) it heals `bossRegenPercentPerSec` 1.5% of its max health a second (~67 s from empty to full), never above max; any hit stops it at once and restarts the wait; paused while paralysed or coughing in the Smoke and while dying. While it regenerates (`boss.regenerating`) its health bar pulses slowly green with a tiny "+" (`DIALOGUE.bossRegenIcon`; `ui.setBossBar(…, regen)`, the `.regen` class). Its attacks and max health are unchanged. Out of play (the girl's scene) the arena lets go of the horse. Reset on Retry, Play Again and the test menu (`arena.reset()`); `arena.sealNow()` starts the sealed fight directly (test menu "Boss fight"). Corner rescue and the camera's slide-in are unchanged.
- Boss (`boss.js`, `CONFIG.boss`): the cave monster (~2.4× AJ, `loadMonsterModel()` — Warrok, see Boss model below), 100 HP, guards the cave mouth until the hero enters the arena; **leashed** to the arena: its centre never gets further than `boss.arenaRadius` 16.5 from the arena's centre (clamped in its walk, the charge, the rescue push and once more at the end of every update), so it can never reach the boulders, the barrier or leave into the cave; health bar top centre. Model: horned head with glowing eyes and a hinged jaw (teeth, tusks), glowing mouth, two jointed arms with huge fists, two jointed legs with clawed feet. States: Guard → Chase → Melee (`CONFIG.boss.melee`: hero within 4 → a swipe or a punch at random, 0.6 s visible wind-up with its facing locked — a dodge roll avoids it — then one heart if the hero is in reach and in front, then a 2 s recovery) or, at mid range only (4 … `slam.range` 4.6), Slam (the jump attack: rears up 0.8 s with both fists overhead over a red ground circle, then smashes both fists into the ground on the circle — damage inside it, expanding shockwave ring, screen shake; it stays planted) or Fire Breath (`CONFIG.boss.fire`, 4.6–10 away, 6 s cooldown: 1 s warning with the head thrown back, jaw open, mouth glowing and a red cone on the ground that slowly follows the hero; then 2 s of fire particles along the locked cone; half a heart (`flameDamageHearts` 0.5) every 0.8 s (`tick`) while the hero stands inside it — no knockback and no post-hit invincibility from a burn, so every tick counts; a sideways dodge gets out) or Charge (head down, 1 s pause, straight rush; into a boulder → stunned 2 s with bonus damage) → Recover (pause after every attack). Below each of `hpMarks` (75 / 50 / 25%) it fires `onHpMark(i)` once per fight: a ring of 6 gems (2 of each colour, `gems.arena`) appears 4 in from the arena's edge, collected by walking over them (toast "Gems at the arena edge!"); the rings hide again when the boss resets. Damage it takes is its own scale (`hitPct`, a share of max HP per hit whatever the hit's number): every combo hit (punches and the kick) 3%, a Batarang 2%, the Flash bolt exactly 40%; stunned ×1.5. Smoke Bomb on the boss (`boss.smoke`): the moment its body touches the cloud it is interrupted — chase, recovery or any wind-up cancelled (the slam's red circle goes) — and coughs, doubled over (state `smoked`, `hit` clip at `model.smokedAt` with a coughing head bob and jaw); inside it creeps toward the hero at 15% speed (`slow` 0.85) and can't leave the cloud before the 0.5 s exposure completes, then paralysis; mid slam landing (`slam`) or charge rush (`charge`) nothing happens until the frame after it ends. Paralysis (`boss.paralysis`): only 4 s (other monsters keep `smokeBomb.paralyze` 10 s; the timer ring counts down from 4), stopping all actions, taking +20% damage; it looks dazed the whole time (`paralysis.dazed`): its idle at half speed (`idleRate` 0.5), the body swaying gently side to side (`sway` / `swayRate`), the head hung low (`headDown`), a green tint and a ring of stars circling and bobbing above its head; when it ends it snaps out of it with a short roar (`wakeRoar` 0.6 s of the `roar` clip, jaw open, inside its usual recovery; `events.onWake` → the `bossRoar` sound placeholder); afterwards it **resists paralysis for 20 s** (`resistsParalysis()`: no inhaling, no effect, toast "It shrugs off the smoke!"). Phase 2 below 50% HP (`enrageAt` 0.5): wind-ups ×0.65 (`enrageTime`), recoveries ×0.5 (`enrageRecover`, the melee's 2 s too), walks ×1.35, and up close it slams instead of swiping/punching 40% of the time (`enrageSlamChance`); every slam **cracks the ground open** — jagged dark fissures with an orange glow that fade after 9 s (`loadCrackModel()`, `boss.crack`, pooled) and `events.onCrack(position)`: slimes rise out of it (see Minions). Hit flash, damage numbers. Dying at the boss uses a respawn, inside the sealed arena (see Sealed arena: full hearts and abilities, 3 s protection, the boss regains only half the damage dealt and backs off to the far side — `boss.reviveRecover`); outside the sealed fight, with `bossResetsOnRespawn`, the boss resets to full health on guard (`boss.onHeroRespawn()`). Defeat: stumbles, falls, fades, "Victory!" banner; quest becomes "Enter the cave"; the girl (`loadGirlModel()` in girl.js) appears at the cave mouth.
- Boss model (`monsterModel.js`, `CONFIG.boss.model`): **Warrok** (Mixamo) from `public/models/boss/boss.glb` (~0.5 MB, converted from the 12 MB `boss.fbx` by `scripts/girl-to-glb.mjs`: skeleton in centimetres under a 0.01 root so the animation FBX files beside it retarget unchanged, textures 1024 px, meshopt — GLTFLoader + `setMeshoptDecoder`; `boss.fbx` and its `boss.fbm/` texture dump are git-ignored). Scaled to `boss.height` 5.0 (~2.4× AJ's 2.07; collision `radius` 1.6); collision radius, HP and the slam / charge / fire timings unchanged. AnimationMixer, 0.2 s crossfades, clip by state: guard/recover → `idle`; melee wind-up + blow → `swipe` or `punch` (scrubbed: `model.swipe` / `model.punch` windows, the blow landing at the hit); chase → `walk` (speed-matched, faster enraged); slam wind-up + slam → `jumpattack` scrubbed so the fists hit the ground on the red circle exactly when the slam lands (`slamWindup`/`slamImpact` windows; the leap is visual, the logical position stays put); fire wind-up → `roar` rearing back with the mouth glowing (`mouth`, an additive glow on the Jaw bone; the jaw opens procedurally), fire → `roar` thrusting the head forward with the flames streaming from the mouth for 2 s; charge wind-up → `roar` (1 s), charge → `run`; stunned → `hit` (doubles over, holds, recovers); paralyzed → `idle` at half speed, the hips swaying and the head hung low on top (`rotateBone`, mixamo.js), green tint + dizzy stars (above the head bone), then `roar` (`model.wakeRoar` clip times) as it snaps out of it; dying → `death` (falls on its back), then the fade. Inhaling the Smoke Bomb heaves the chest bone. The loading bar covers the boss (`heroModel.loadShares.boss`); if it fails to load, the placeholder monster is used.
- Corner trap (`rescue.js`, `CONFIG.corner`): a cornered hero always has a way out. (a) The dodge roll passes through slimes and the boss and bends away from rocks / walls (see Combat). (b) Slimes are pushed out of a 1.5 `slimes.exclusion` round the hero (softly at `push`, shoved at `dodgeShove` by a roll) and none stays inside longer than `maxInside` 0.5 s (`minions.#clearOfHero`). (c) Pinned — within `pinGap` of a rock / wall surface (`world.solidsNear`) with an enemy's body within `enemyRange` 3 — for `pinTime` 1.5 s on foot: a rescue pulse (`CornerRescue.pulse`): minions within `pushRadius` knocked back `minionPush` (slimes hop away), the boss pushed back `bossPush` inside the arena and into its usual recovery (`boss.rescueRecover`: any wind-up or attack cancelled; already stunned / paralyzed / coughing / staggered it is only pushed; HP, damage and timings untouched), an expanding cyan ring and a bright shell (`loadRescuePulseModel()`), a screen shake and the `rescuePulse` sound placeholder, then `cornerRescueCooldown` 15 s. On horseback (`corner.mounted`): with `count` 2 or more ground enemies within `range` 3 of the horse for more than `time` 2 s, the horse rears up (`horse.rearUp()`, a nose-up pitch, the riders go with it) and a shockwave (the same ring, a shake, the `rearUp` sound placeholder) knocks enemies within `knockRadius` back and defeats skeletons within `defeatRadius` (gems drop); then `mountedRescueCooldown` 10 s. The boss's body push on the hero is resolved against the world again (never into a rock). Reset on respawn, Retry and a new game.
- Minions (`minions.js`, `minionModels.js`, `CONFIG.minions`): small enemies from the Quaternius Animated Monster Pack (`public/models/monsters/*.fbx`, CC0; see CREDITS.md) — bat, skeleton, slime. Each type's FBX loads lazily the first time one spawns (`loadMinionTemplate`, cached; spawns are immediate once loaded), scaled to `fit`, textures ≤ 1024 px, a SkeletonUtils clone with its own materials and mixer per spawn, root x/z travel held (the game moves them); if a file fails, a code-built placeholder (flapping bat, bone-white skeleton, squashing slime). The main terrain grid covers x −150…150; the desert has its own coarser grid (`loadDesertTerrainModel`). The river (x≈0, meandering) runs the whole length of the map. Boundary hills on all edges.
- Layout is generated deterministically from `CONFIG.world.seed` in `world.js` (`generateLayout`). Terrain height is analytic: `world.heightAt(x,z)`.
- Colliders in `world.js`: `boxes` (buildings: standable tops + walls), `beams` (horizontal branches: flat walkable top that tapers base→tip, solid sides), `cylinders` (trunks, rocks, forest trees). Hero uses `world.groundAt()` and `world.collide()`.
- Tree-leaping zone: each route platform is a thick limb that grows sideways out of a bowed, root-flared trunk (an elbow) and then runs along the route, wide base facing the previous platform. Twigs angle outward off the path; leaf clusters (3 greens) stay ≥ `leafLiftMin` above the walkable top. Background giant trees frame the swamp. No floating platforms.
- Tree-zone visibility: leaf clusters (separate transparent foliage material, per-instance `instanceFade`) that block the camera→hero line or sit within `CONFIG.foliage.fadeRadius` of the hero fade smoothly to ~15% (`World.updateFoliage`). Trunks/branches stay solid. Canopy over the path is kept small, pushed to the sides and above the jump apex; crowns lean away from the walking line.
- Jump guide (`guides.js`): while airborne a ring marks the spot directly below the hero (yellow over a platform, red over the swamp/street); the next branch on the route gets a soft pulsing glow.
- Camera: in the tree zone it pulls back 20% and looks further down (blended at the zone edges). It never renders from inside trunks, rocks, buildings or leaf clusters: snaps in front of solids, eases back out; leaves merely in the way fade instead.
- Trail corridor: a tree-free corridor 22 wide (`track.corridorHalfWidth` = 11) along the whole spline. Invisible walls (`track.wall`, offset 7.3 from the centre line) keep the hero and horse on the 14-wide trail: `World.collide` pushes back and removes only the outward velocity, the horse rails along them (turns to run with the trail, loses a little speed), and the camera never goes beyond them. Walls start 10 into the trail (the horse's spot) and end 25 before the arena. A continuous dense line of bushes and boulders (`track.edge`, not colliders, skipped in the river) runs along both corridor edges, wholly outside it. Forest trees are placed only by sampling the spline — rows in a dense band just outside the corridor plus a sparser outer band (`forest.near*`, `farBand`), each tree (crown included) wholly outside the corridor; three species (conifer, broadleaf, birch) with varied size, stretch and tint, chunked by `forest.chunkSize` so far chunks cull and skip the fade update. Each species is two InstancedMeshes per chunk: solid trunks + fadeable crowns. All foliage (giant-tree leaves and forest crowns) lives in `world.foliageSets` and uses the same fade and camera collision.
- Ride checkpoints (`CONFIG.checkpoints.fractions`): the horse's spot plus every 25% of the trail (moved forward off the river and past any obstacle or wall run-up), flag poles at the trail's right edge; flag turns green when reached. Respawning faces along the trail.
- Repeated objects use `InstancedMesh` (buildings, roofs, giant-tree trunks/limbs/twigs/leaves, forest species, rocks, clouds). Building windows are drawn in-shader in world space.
- Quests (`quests.js`): ordered objectives with a target + completion test, HUD text with distance (the quest panel top left) — no arrow, beacon or other 3D marker or pointer in the world. Current: "Jump across the rooftops" → "Leap through the trees" → "Mount the horse" (target follows the horse) → "Ride to the cave" (distance left along the trail; done at the auto-dismount) → "Defeat the monster" → "Enter the cave" (target: the girl; done when she joins) → "Ride to the castle" (distance along the desert trail) → done at the ending.
