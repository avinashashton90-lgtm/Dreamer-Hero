# Debug tools, test commands and playtest

- Debug keys: G grants all gems, H damages the hero, T teleports to the arena entrance, X kills the boss, B spawns a bat right ahead (`minions.debugBat()`), P jumps to the girl's cutscene, O to the desert ride, L to the Lazarus Pit ("Stop."), U to the castle (gate cinematic → ending). With Alt (`CONFIG.input.altDebugKeys`; K, S and G alone are dodge, move back and grant gems): Alt+K spawns a skeleton group (`minions.skeletonGroup()`), Alt+S toggles the storm, Alt+G jumps to the castle-gate cinematic.
- Hidden test menu (`testMenu.js`, `CONFIG.testMenu`, labels in `DIALOGUE.testMenu`): open the game with **`?debug`** at the end of the URL (e.g. `https://…/Dreamer-Hero/?debug`; normal players never see it), **or hold the HUD speaker button for `holdSeconds` 3 s** (a ring fills while held; a `vibrate` buzz and a "Test menu on" / "Test menu off" toast confirm) — that switch is saved in localStorage (`storageKey`, try/catch) until it is held again (`testMenuSaved` / `saveTestMenu`, `TestMenu.setEnabled`). A red **TEST** button (70 × 40, thumb-sized) sits top-left under the quest bar, right of the hearts row (no overlap with the hearts, the horse's energy bar or the quest panel); tapping it opens a panel of big buttons in two sections. **Part 1**: Forest start, Forest hurdles (on the horse `hurdleApproach` 30 before the first hurdle — right at it, in view), Cave entrance, Boss fight (straight into the sealed arena: `arena.sealNow()` — AJ inside, the barrier shut, the horse by the entrance, Amy watching), After boss (Amy rescued, riding to the desert), Lazarus Pit scene, Desert chase (right after the pit, skeletons 2 s later), Sandstorm (the chase with the storm at full strength), Castle gate cinematic. **Part 2**: Portal scene (`story.debugPortal()`: inside the castle, gates shut → Amy and the portal → Part 2), Gearhaven arrival, Fire / Lightning / Steam / Magnet / Time level (stubs: "coming soon"), Amy's final fight (a stub), Part 2 ending, Robots test (Tick and the four mech robots in a row, each playing its clips), Gearhaven ride (on the horse at the ride's start), **Props test** (`enter({ start: 'props' })`, `showProps()`, propsTest.js, `part2.propsTest`: on its own brass strip away from the courtyard, every kind of kit prop in a row with a label each — gear_01, the gear cluster, a Factory Kit cog, the collectible gear, the saw (sliding, spinning), a conveyor (slats moving), a pipe stack puffing steam, a catwalk, the Coaster Kit's Monorail and Steel rails side by side on their supports, the clock tower, the Castle Kit gate (its leaves opening and shutting) — the camera sliding along the row and back, a swipe moves it). Then **Give 14 gears** (the gear rewards' first tier; onto the ride first if not on it; press again for the next), **Look: <name>** (cycles Gearhaven's look — Verdigris Dusk → Midnight Clockwork → Sunrise Brass — at once if Part 2 is loaded, else for when it loads; the panel stays open, the label shows the new one), Audio test (a panel with every sound, the music sections and layers, the voice blips; no jump) — and Close (section headings: `{ header }` items in `TestMenu`). Each one leaves whatever is running (intro, cutscene, Game Over, ending, Part 2 — `levels.load('part1')` first) for a fresh game (`story.resetAll()`, as Play Again), jumps with the existing helpers (`horse.restartRide`, the T arena teleport `teleportArena()`, `story.debugDesert` / `debugPit` / `debugGate` / `finish`, the pit scene skipped with `cinematic.skip()` for the chase), then gives full hearts, full horse energy and `abilityUses` 5 uses of every ability (unlocked). AJ is on the horse (on foot for the boss fight, since the arena dismounts him) with Amy behind him from "After boss" on. In the desert before the pit the story's exhaustion still applies (slump, slower horse, no ability buttons). Touch works without a keyboard: the touch router ignores taps on `.test-menu` and `.audio-ui` (input.js), so they arrive as plain clicks.
