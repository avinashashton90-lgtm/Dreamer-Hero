# Dreamer Hero story bible
- Part 1: AJ, an average schoolboy, dreams he is a hero. Forest ride, gems unlock abilities, cave boss, rescues Amy, desert, Lazarus Pit (hearts restored; Amy: "Thank you. But... who are you?" and only smiles), castle gate.
- Amy secret: she was the Clockmaker's apprentice and keeper of the Dream Clock. Trying to stop the clock from running down, she used forbidden magic. It went wrong: her magic was sealed in five elements (Fire, Lightning, Steam/Wind, Magnet, Time), scattered, and she was cast out into AJ's world with no memory.
- Part 2 (Gearhaven, a clockwork world built around the Dream Clock; if it stops, AJ's dream ends): at the castle gate Amy's memory and dark magic return; a portal pulls AJ and the horse in. Guide: Tick, a small brass robot. AJ collects the five elements one per level; each gives a new ability replacing gems. Amy looks like the villain for most of Part 2 (steals Lightning, hurts Tick). Final fight: AJ must reflect her magic with the right element. At the end her seal cracks and her memory returns; AJ lowers his weapon instead of killing her. The clock restarts. Amy says his name for the first time, but a dark shimmer stays in her hand. Ends: "To be continued... Part 3".
- Part 3: Amy's dark side returns; magic fight, floating, sword monsters, an armoured mech boss at the castle (George, the biggest Quaternius mech — it replaces the turtle mech), the clockwork dragon (already held).
- Tone: AJ is a dreamer, not a fighter. Some challenges are solved with imagination. Amy is cold in the middle of Part 2, never friendly, so the turn lands.
- Do not use the words "princess" or "Mars" for Amy or Gearhaven.
- Fire Element level (built): the furnace city after the Gearhaven ride — conveyors, steam vents and rails, seven cog keys (two on the main line; five near the trail), scout robots (Leela) in pairs, a plaza with chests, patrolling sword robots (Mike, Stan), then the Furnace Guardian (George, tinted hot red-orange) in a sealed circular plaza. Six keys cover the two-key gate and all four chests (two small, a big one with a heart container, and the Shield chest); each chest costs one key. AJ starts the level with neither Fire Blast nor the Shield: the Shield is found in the plaza's Shield chest (raised for a moment, it blocks the Guardian's Steam Burst, Charge and Fire Ball, and a block tapped just as the hit lands is a perfect block that staggers him; the Stomp can only be jumped). When the Guardian falls the Fire Element rises on a pedestal; AJ takes it (his eyes flash orange) and only then gets Fire Blast. Tick: "That is one of five." / AJ: "Where are the others?" / Tick: "Wherever she has not taken them yet." Then a hooded figure in violet is seen on a distant tower: Tick "Someone is watching us." AJ "Amy?" Tick "I cannot say." — she vanishes in a puff of violet light.
- Items (part2/items.js, reusable by every level): chest (Fire level: 1 cog key each), cogKey (a large golden cog, separate from the small brass gears; seven in the Fire level), elixirHeal (green, +2 hearts), elixirEnergy (blue, refills the horse), gem (blue-yellow crystal: 1 gem = 1 Batarang throw), heartContainer (+1 max heart, from a big chest), gearBundle, runeStone (a stone slab with a glowing symbol — for later levels), wandPiece (for later).
- Wand-piece plan: three wand pieces, one hidden in each of three later levels (Lightning, Magnet, Time — found in the level's chests or by a rune-stone puzzle), form Amy's wand. Assembled, the wand is what lets AJ reflect her magic in the final fight and, at the end, what her seal cracks from; the shimmer left in her hand after Part 2 is the wand's last piece (Part 3). Rune stones open the way to the pieces.
- Elements row (HUD): five diamond slots — flame (Fire), bolt (Lightning), swirl (Steam), magnet (Magnet), clock hand (Time) — grey outlines until recovered, then glowing in the element's colour.
- Lightning Element level (built, P2-G; the Fire level's machinery with its settings laid over it): the same city road with eight tesla coils zapping a lane on a timer (half a heart; jump or steer round), scouts and sword robots, blue gems, one small chest, then the TESLA GUARDIAN (George tinted blue-violet: Stomp, a Lightning Lane, Overcharge — the charge, a pillar stuns him 3 s). Story beat at the orb: Amy (hooded in violet, no glow) appears, blasts it, snatches most of it, hits Tick so he tumbles and dims, and vanishes; one shard flies into AJ's hand — Lightning is his (Spark Chain: up to three foes in range, 6 s). Tick, hurt: "She... took it." AJ: "Why would she do this?"
- Steam Element level (built, P2-G): a rail-chase on five high rails over the city with steam-jump gaps, scouts from the front, green potions (heal 2 hearts), one cog chest with WAND PIECE 1/3 (HUD: "Wand piece 1/3"), violet bolts from Amy in the sky that the horse must dodge, no boss; the last steam jump flies into a wind current and the horse glides to the Steam orb. Steam is his (Steam Gust: a cone that pushes foes back and lifts the horse into a 3 s glide, 8 s). Tick recovers.
